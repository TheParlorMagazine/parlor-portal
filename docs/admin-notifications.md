# Owner signup and shop notifications

Three independent operational email automations send to **lindsey@theparlormagazine.com** using the existing Resend sender:

- `admin_free_signup`: a verified new free email or Google account. This is independent of the member's welcome email and newsletter subscription state. Existing logins, recovery links, older/migrated accounts, paid members and Google identities linked later to an existing account do not qualify. Email confirmation must happen within 24 hours of account creation; Google initial registration within ten minutes. Registrations predating October 29, 2026 are excluded.
- `admin_paid_subscription`: a newly completed live, paid subscription checkout after the member's paid access has been saved. It includes member name/email, plan, actual checkout amount/currency and subscription ID. Trials, zero-paid checkout and test-mode purchases do not alert. Invoice renewals do not trigger it. A new Stripe subscription gets one notification, not one per webhook delivery.
- `admin_shop_purchase`: a saved live paid shop order, including Trick-or-Treat. It includes every stored cart line, quantities, variants, actual paid total, shipping charge, shipping address and an expanded packing list distinguishing Printify and self-fulfilled components, bundle names and SKUs. Zero-priced Trick-or-Treat line allocations say “Included in bundle”; the paid order total is authoritative. No new digital article/audio/video owner alert is added in this change.

No new migration, secret, external webhook or subscriber consent change is needed. These hooks use the existing `claim_stripe_email` RPC and `stripe_email_deliveries` table (despite the name, its keys accept non-Stripe signup IDs). Unique keys are the member ID, Stripe subscription ID or paid checkout session ID plus automation type and owner recipient. Resend receives an additional idempotency key. Failed duplicate-protection checks never send, uncertain delivery claims are preserved, and explicit rejections follow the existing retry rules.

Signup alert errors are caught so they do not interrupt authentication or redirects. For shop return-page processing, an alert failure does not reverse a saved order. Stripe webhook failures can retry delivery after durable fulfillment, with customer receipts and Printify orders retaining their existing duplicate protection. Admin alerts do not create purchases, send products or change subscription status.

The three controls appear in **Admin → Emails → Automations**, enabled by default. Template overrides can use `{{notification}}` to preserve the complete dynamic notification body; replacing it with static text intentionally replaces the details. Existing customer receipts and unsubscribe links are unchanged. No real email is sent during tests, and no historical member/order backfill is run. Publishing enables the alerts for future qualifying callbacks/webhooks; old fulfilled/cancelled shop order replays remain unchanged.
